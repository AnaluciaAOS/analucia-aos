Privacy, in plain words

What this site does

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It loads nothing from other sites: no fonts, no scripts, no images, no counters and no trackers. Every file comes from this site.
  • It has no forms. It does not ask you for any information.

If you write to us

The address hello@analuciaaos.com is used only to answer your message. We do not add you to a list, and we do not share your message with anyone.

If you choose to talk to us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms.

Removing your messages

To ask us to delete the messages you sent and our replies, write to hello@analuciaaos.com.

What the hosting company sees

Like any website, the server that hosts this site may keep technical records of visits (for example the time of a request and the address it came from). This page does not add anything to that.
